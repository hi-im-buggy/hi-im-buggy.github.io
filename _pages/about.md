I'm a student researcher working as a research assistant at the [University of Jyväskylä (JYU)](https://jyu.fi),
under the supervision of [Petri Toivainen](http://users.jyu.fi/~ptoiviai/). My work at JYU is on concept-based interpretability
of deep neural networks for music, specifically looking at the causal relationships between lower-level concepts like instruments
and higher-level concepts like genre.

I'm working my Master's thesis at [IIIT Hyderabad](https://iiit.ac.in), advised by [Vinoo Alluri](https://www.iiit.ac.in/faculty/vinoo-alluri/)
and [Makarand Tapaswi](https://makarandtapaswi.github.io/).

My research is primarily concerned with how deep neural networks process and organize information for auditory tasks.
I'm also interested in interpretability and auditory processing at large, multi-modal deep learning in vision-language
and audio-language models.
